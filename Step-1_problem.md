A. Within this problem the requirements are that when a train is approaching the crossing is closed whereas if either no train is coming or a vehicle is still on the tracks  the crossing is open.
B. The inputs for this system are the approaching train and or the vehicle on the tracks. The outputs are the arms of the crossing being raised or lowered depending on the inputs of the system.
C. constraints of such a system without knowing the area or economic status of it. The constraints would consist of the signal of the approaching train being either from a weight based trigger or a radar/camera which for the weight trigger could be set off by some large debris or other such heavy object being on the tracks, a similar issue would be for the sensor to track if there is a vehicle on the track. Another constraint of this would be the theoretical cost of such a system being installed and maintained as each input and output would need constant maintenance to ensure the safety of the crossing.
