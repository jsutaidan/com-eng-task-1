The system that was widely adopted in Europe, the Siemens level crossing system utilises sensors, signal lights, barriers being controlled by CENELEC certified control logic to safely manage the train crossings and road traffic at these crossings. It uses track circuits to detect train approach and departure which triggers the lowering and raising of the gates as well as the signal lights to better guide and protect traffic. Siemens uses modular designs to better fit the various needs of the European market of railway environments. This system could easily be used as a basis for the design of the project’s train crossing system with minor changes based on any requirements of the individual crossing.
